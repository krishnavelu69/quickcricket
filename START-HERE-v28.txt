EDIT YOUR SHOP
Sports Shop -> Manage my shop -> Online business profiles -> Edit business.
Public: display name, city/area, description, logo and cover.
Private: shop contact number and full verification address.
Enable Show this sports shop in the public shop directory and Save changes.
Then Sports Shop -> Refresh products -> Browse sports shops.
Product publication remains separate from shop-directory publication.
Do not enter private contact/address details in the public description, area or images.

VERIFICATION FOR THE PLATFORM OWNER
In your Supabase project -> Table Editor -> qc_merchant_profiles:
review contact and verification_address. These fields are accessible to the merchant
owning the record and authorised database administrators, not public shoppers or other merchants.
This update collects verification details; it does not add an in-app administrator portal,
approval status, verification badge or a requirement for approval before publishing.
Existing shops remain intact. Add their full address when you next edit them.
